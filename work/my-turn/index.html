My Turn

Career design as a retention strategy

A career design program for post-COVID attrition — toolkits that help managers keep people, and an employee portal for growth and learning.

Career designRetentionJob crafting

The problem

After COVID, people were leaving. The usual answer — more benefits — doesn't touch the reason most people go: they can't see their next step where they are.

What we designed

Two sides of the same program. For managers, toolkits for retention conversations and job crafting. For employees, a portal for growth and learning, so the next step inside the company is visible.

The idea underneath

Retention is a product problem: make the internal path easier to see and take than the external one.
