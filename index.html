02 Rafael · Learning product Safety Escape Room Safety training you can't pass by guessing ProblemMandatory safety training measured attendance, not understanding.MoveA digital escape room: hazards randomized each run, a pass threshold at the exit.ResultAssessment built into the experience; the certificate issues itself on completion.

Safety Escape Room

The problem

Mandatory safety training tends to measure attendance, not understanding. A fixed quiz can be passed by remembering the answers.

What I built

A digital escape room for Rafael's safety engineers. Each run randomizes the hazards, so the only way out is to recognise them. A pass threshold gates the exit, and completion issues the certificate automatically, with no manual step.

Why it matters

The same principle as PLAYGROUND: put the learner inside the situation, and let the structure of the game do the assessing.

My Turn

The problem

After COVID, people were leaving. The usual answer, more benefits, doesn't touch the reason most people go: they can't see their next step where they are.

What we designed

Two sides of the same program. For managers, toolkits for retention conversations and job crafting. For employees, a portal for growth and learning, so the next step inside the company is visible.

The idea underneath

Retention is a product problem: make the internal path easier to see and take than the external one.
